## Channel architecture observed
A recurring commercial architecture is visible:

SOCIAL DISCOVERY
-> Facebook / TikTok / Instagram / creator content
-> catalog or link hub
-> WhatsApp advisor or WhatsApp catalog
-> stock/price confirmation
-> payment
-> physical pickup or national shipping
-> warranty/support

This pattern should be treated as a real Bolivia commercial architecture hypothesis and measured further.

## Public buyer-friction signals
Public conversations are anecdotal and biased; they are recorded as signals, not population facts.

### TRUST
Observed buyers ask whether a store physically exists, whether it is safe to transfer money, and whether unusually low prices indicate fraud.

### PHYSICAL PROOF
Physical store/location is repeatedly used by users as a trust heuristic.

### ORIGINAL / NEW / REFURBISHED / FAKE
Public conversations report concern that products sold as original/new can be fake, used or refurbished.

### WARRANTY
Buyers compare local stores with foreign/import options partly on warranty and post-sale handling.

### EXACT VARIANT
Buyers care about exact GPU/memory/model/variant and fear receiving a substitute or mismatched product.

### LANDED COST
Buyers frequently do not know whether US price + exchange + shipping + customs + fees will actually beat local price.

### IMPORT COMPLEXITY
Questions recur about Amazon/eBay/AliExpress, couriers, customs, timing and risk.

### PRODUCT FIT
Architecture, engineering, programming, design, gaming and content-creation use cases appear in both customer questions and competitor marketing.

### CITY / NATIONAL ACCESS
Buyers regularly ask about interdepartmental shipment and compare stores from other cities rather than limiting purchase to their own city.

## Strategic hypothesis emerging
A potentially stronger IPCENTER proposition is not "cheapest importer" and not simply "we recommend a laptop".

The stronger hypothesis is:
VERIFIED NATIONAL SOURCING + PRODUCT FIT + EXACT-VARIANT CONTROL + LANDED-COST TRANSPARENCY + PROVENANCE + WARRANTY CLARITY + NATIONAL FULFILLMENT.

This is not yet approved positioning; it requires validation against first-party IPCENTER history and controlled commercial tests.
