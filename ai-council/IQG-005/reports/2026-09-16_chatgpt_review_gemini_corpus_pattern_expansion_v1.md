## Material corrections

### 1. CEO_REPORTED is not an internal record

The historical nationwide shipping and MSI Leopard sales are currently `CEO_REPORTED`, not `High (Internal Record)`.

Correct state:
`CEO_REPORTED / HISTORICAL_FIRST_PARTY_TO_RECOVER`.

### 2. Four budget observations are not a "tiered stratification"

Gemini turned ~Bs 3,100, ~Bs 8,000, ~Bs 12,000–13,000 and ~Bs 15,000 into discrete budget clusters.

This is overreach. They are four public observations, not a distribution.

Use:
`OBSERVED_BUDGET_EXAMPLES`, not `BUDGET_TIERS`, until larger data exists.

### 3. Geographic map contains unsupported context

Downgrade/reject as unsupported unless independently sourced:
- higher market density / competitive pricing in Santa Cruz,
- high administrative and technical-student density in La Paz,
- institutional/mining baseline for Sucre/Potosí,
- border-trade awareness and Argentine competition in Tarija,
- high logistics premium / frontier trading economy in Trinidad/Cobija,
- mining-economy cash-flow patterns in Oruro/Llallagua.

These may be plausible, but the corpus did not establish them.

### 4. Category gap map contains unverified commercial claims

Downgrade to hypothesis:
- strong RTX 4060/4070 demand,
- buyers will pay Bs 8k–15k for exact fit,
- B2B procurement is underserved,
- importers capture high margins on niche products,
- companies prefer local trusted entities over anonymous couriers.

### 5. Emerging-demand method uses arbitrary windows

The proposed:
- rolling 14-day windows,
- top 50 competitors,
- additions within 7 days,
are `TO_CALIBRATE`, not operational rules.

### 6. Search backlog is valuable but some tasks are confirmation-biased

Several "expected evidence" fields assume the answer in advance.

Examples requiring neutral rewrite:
- query 28 assumes local stock commands a legitimate premium;
- query 31 preloads a 24–48h shipping expectation;
- query 39 preloads 30–50% reservation deposits as standard practice;
- query 40 assumes altitude/power-grid concerns affect battery longevity;
- query 50 presupposes a premium-chassis strategy.

Search must be allowed to return "no evidence" or contradict the hypothesis.

### 7. Stop conditions are sampling rules, not truth thresholds

Numbers like 10 stores, 8 samples, 5 threads, 3 couriers are convenient collection targets only.

Label:
`SAMPLING_TARGET`, not evidence sufficiency.

### 8. Hypothesis section contains unsupported numerical and causal claims

Reject/downgrade:
- local warranty beats a 10% cheaper import,
- store address + NIT eliminates 80% of trust friction,
- delivery under 48h drives regional surcharge willingness,
- 30–50% deposit filters time-wasters,
- Bs 3,100 is a "premium smartphone buyer tier",
- February–March and July–August demand surges,
- historical MSI Leopard sales prove premium-chassis demand,
- historical shipping proves regional viability.

These are hypotheses, not findings.

### 9. Public awareness/engagement state needs caution

Likes/comments/shares can show exposure or interaction, not necessarily buyer awareness or unique human users. The buyer-state model should preserve signal quality and deduplication.

### 10. Historical recovery blueprint is useful and should be prioritized

This is one of Gemini's strongest contributions. Historical first-party records can validate:
- city,
- product,
- variant,
- actual paid amount,
- shipping method,
- delivery outcome,
- repeat purchase,
- warranty incident.

Use explicit transaction keys for matching; do not infer cross-source identity from phone/name alone when ambiguous.
