## Canonical objective
IPCenter is national-scope. We are not optimizing for Cochabamba-only. The research question is:

**Who can actually buy, what do they want to buy, where do they want to buy it, what price/budget is acceptable, and what friction prevents purchase?**

The system must make this easy for the customer using real evidence, not invented demand.

### Evidence hierarchy for buyability
TRANSACTION > DEPOSIT/PREPAYMENT > ACCEPTED QUOTE > EXPLICIT BUDGET + CONCRETE NEED > EXPLICIT PRODUCT REQUEST > SOCIAL INTEREST.

Never infer individual wealth from appearance, lifestyle, politics, health, ethnicity, neighborhood alone, or social-media aesthetics. Territorial purchasing-power data may be used only as aggregate context.
